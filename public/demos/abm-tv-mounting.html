Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

Do I need to provide the TV mount?
No! We bring professional-grade mounts with us. Our mounts are included in our pricing and are rated for your specific TV size. If you already have a mount you'd like us to use, just let us know.
How long does installation take?
Most standard TV mounting jobs take 1-2 hours. Fireplace mounts and full wire concealment may take 2-3 hours. We'll give you an accurate time estimate when you call.
What types of walls can you mount on?
We mount on drywall, plaster, brick, stone, and concrete walls. We always anchor to wall studs for maximum security. For brick/concrete, we use specialized masonry anchors.
Can you mount a TV above my fireplace?
Absolutely! Fireplace mounts are one of our specialties. We use heat-resistant mounts and can install tilting or pull-down brackets for optimal viewing angles.
Do you hide the wires?
Yes! We offer full in-wall wire concealment for a clean, professional look. This involves running cables through the wall so you see no visible wires. It's included in our Full Concealment package.
What areas do you service?
We serve all of Greater Houston including Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Pearland, Cypress, Spring, Humble, Pasadena, League City, Missouri City, and surrounding areas.
Do you offer same-day service?
Yes! We offer same-day service based on availability. Call or text us and we'll do our best to get your TV mounted today. We respond to all inquiries within 15 minutes.
Is my TV too big/heavy to mount?
We mount TVs of all sizes, from 32" to 85" and larger. Our professional-grade mounts can support even the heaviest TVs when properly anchored to wall studs.
