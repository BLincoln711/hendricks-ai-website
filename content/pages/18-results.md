# Search systems should produce measurable decisions and outcomes.

Every Hendricks case study states:

- Starting condition
- Business question
- Baseline
- Intervention
- Timeframe
- Measurement source
- Observed result
- Attribution approach
- Limitations
- Evidence grade

## Card requirements

Each case-study card shows:

- Verified status
- Industry
- Problem
- One approved outcome
- Timeframe
- Evidence grade
- Link

## Publication rules

Never publish:

- A percentage with no baseline
- A client logo without permission
- An anonymous result that cannot be verified internally
- “AI visibility increased” with no definition
- “Revenue influenced” with no methodology
- A testimonial with no role or relationship
- A generated dashboard presented as client data

## Empty-state rule

Do not display an empty Results page or “coming soon” cards in primary navigation. Keep the route disabled until proof exists.
