# Submit a Privacy Request

Use this form to request access to, correction of, deletion of, portability of, restriction of, or another applicable action concerning personal information maintained by Hendricks.

Hendricks may request additional information when reasonably necessary to verify your identity, locate relevant records, protect against fraud, or confirm that an authorized agent may act for you. Do not submit government identification, financial-account information, health information, passwords, or other sensitive material unless Hendricks specifically requests it through a secure method.

This form is not for service inquiries. For a commercial inquiry, use the Contact or Search Intelligence Diagnostic form.

## Request form fields

### Relationship to Hendricks

Required single select:

- Website visitor
- Business contact or prospect
- Marketing subscriber
- Current or former client contact
- Agency-partner contact
- Authorized agent
- Other

### Request type

Required multi-select:

- Confirm whether Hendricks processes my personal information
- Access or receive a copy
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete information
- Obtain portable information
- Restrict or object to processing
- Withdraw marketing consent
- Appeal a prior privacy-request decision
- Ask a privacy question
- Other applicable request

### Contact and verification information

- First name
- Last name
- Email address
- Company, if relevant
- State, province, or country of residence
- Previous email address used with Hendricks, if different
- Request details

### Authorized-agent fields

Show only when “Authorized agent” is selected:

- Consumer's name
- Consumer's email address
- Agent's name
- Agent's organization
- Description of authority
- Secure authorization-document upload only if implemented and approved

Do not request an authorization document through ordinary email.

## Required attestation

Unchecked checkbox:

> I certify that the information in this request is accurate and that I am the person identified in the request or am authorized to submit the request on that person's behalf.

## Notice at collection

> Hendricks will use the information submitted through this form to verify, evaluate, document, and respond to your privacy request; prevent fraud and abuse; comply with applicable law; and maintain legally required records. Hendricks may share the information with service providers that support secure form processing, hosting, email, records management, legal compliance, and identity verification. Do not include sensitive information unless Hendricks specifically requests it through a secure method. Learn more in the [Privacy Notice](/privacy).

## Accessibility and security

- Give the form an accessible error summary.
- Preserve submitted non-sensitive values after validation errors.
- Rate-limit the endpoint.
- Use a honeypot and minimum-submit-time defense.
- Do not use inaccessible CAPTCHA.
- Do not send request contents to analytics.
- Do not expose request details in URLs.
- Encrypt data in transit.
- Limit internal access.
- Generate a non-sequential confirmation number.
