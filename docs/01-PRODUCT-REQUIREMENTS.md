# 01 — Product Requirements

## 1. Product summary

Rebuild `hendricks.ai` as the website for a premium **Search Intelligence Engineering** firm.

The site must create and explain a differentiated category while remaining commercially understandable. It must convert qualified companies and agencies into fixed-scope Search Intelligence Diagnostic inquiries.

## 2. Business objectives

### Primary

- Establish Hendricks as a credible category leader in Search Intelligence Engineering.
- Make the offer understandable without requiring a live explanation from Brandon.
- Generate qualified Search Intelligence Diagnostic applications.
- Create a clear path from Diagnostic to implementation and managed services.
- Demonstrate technical, search, analytics, and business-measurement credibility.

### Secondary

- Create source-ready category and methodology pages that can earn citations and links.
- Support agency-partner inquiries without confusing the direct-brand proposition.
- Provide a scalable publishing foundation for Hendricks research and verified case studies.
- Preserve useful authority from the existing Hendricks.ai domain during migration.

### Not launch objectives

- Launching a self-service SaaS platform.
- Selling autonomous AI agents as a primary service.
- Building a prompt tracker to compete with existing AI-visibility tools.
- Creating a high-volume content farm.
- Incorporating The Search Economy as a Hendricks solution or content hub.

## 3. Core market thesis

Traditional search was largely organized as:

**Query → Search results → Website → Conversion**

AI-mediated search increasingly performs more of the journey:

**Need → Intent interpretation → Research → Comparison → Synthesis → Shortlist → Choice**

The resulting business problem is:

> **Brands are losing control over the path between being discovered and being chosen.**

The implication:

> A brand can have a strong website, rankings, reviews, paid media, and recognition—and still fail to enter the consideration set during an AI-assisted buying decision.

## 6. Audiences

### Primary audience: brands and companies

#### Decision-makers

- Chief Marketing Officer
- VP or Head of Growth
- VP or Head of Digital
- VP or Head of Demand Generation
- Director or Head of Search
- SEO leader
- Performance marketing leader
- Marketing analytics leader
- Founder or operator of a high-value service business

#### Typical pains

- Search, paid media, content, analytics, AI visibility, PR, and CRM operate separately.
- Competitors appear in AI recommendations and leadership cannot explain why.
- The team tracks prompt mentions without knowing which questions have commercial value.
- Strong expertise exists, but the website and third-party evidence fail to communicate it.
- Reporting stops at traffic, leads, or screenshots.
- Search investment cannot be connected to pipeline with enough confidence.
- Leadership wants a practical AI-search strategy without abandoning proven search fundamentals.

### Secondary audience: agencies

#### Decision-makers

- Agency founder
- Managing director
- Head of SEO
- Head of strategy
- Head of analytics
- Head of innovation or AI
- Client services leader

#### Typical pains

- Clients ask advanced AI-search questions the agency cannot answer confidently.
- Building an internal research, data, measurement, and engineering practice is expensive.
- Off-the-shelf AI-visibility software creates reports but not differentiated strategy.
- The agency needs white-label or embedded specialist support without losing the client relationship.

## 7. Ideal client profile

Strong-fit clients have most of these characteristics:

- Search materially influences a high-value shortlist, appointment, demo, transaction, or account.
- One new customer has meaningful economic value.
- The market has measurable search demand.
- The company has enough authority to change its website, content, profiles, measurement, or campaigns.
- The company can provide Search Console, analytics, paid-search, and CRM access where available.
- Leadership accepts experimentation and evidence limitations.
- The business can create or obtain real proof, not just make claims.

Potential verticals:

- B2B software and technology
- Professional services
- Multi-location and high-value services
- Legal services
- Specialized healthcare
- Wealth management and financial services
- Commercial and high-value real estate
- Enterprise agencies and consultancies

## 8. Poor-fit prospects

- Low-value transactions with little search influence.
- No access to measurement or implementation.
- Desire for guaranteed AI citations or rankings.
- Desire for hundreds of generic AI-written articles.
- A two-week expectation for measurable revenue impact.
- No willingness to identify a target customer or commercial outcome.
- No ability to support claims with evidence.
- A need that can be solved by a simple freelancer or low-cost monitoring tool.

## 9. Customer jobs to be done

A qualified buyer is hiring Hendricks to:

- Determine which search and AI questions actually matter commercially.
- Understand whether a brand is being accurately represented and considered.
- Identify where competitors are winning and what observable evidence surrounds those wins.
- Prioritize the technical, content, authority, evidence, paid, organic, and conversion changes worth implementing.
- Coordinate fragmented search disciplines around one demand model.
- Build a defensible measurement system.
- Give leadership a clear answer about what changed, what should happen next, and how confident the evidence is.

## 10. Offer definitions

### Search Intelligence Diagnostic

A fixed-scope, typically three-to-four-week engagement that maps commercial demand, establishes an observed selection baseline, identifies the Commercial Selection Gap, evaluates measurement readiness, and delivers a prioritized 90-day roadmap.

Recommended price positioning: **$15,000–$25,000**.

### 90-Day Demand-to-Selection Program

A defined implementation program that addresses priority technical, entity, content, evidence, authority, acquisition, conversion, and measurement gaps.

Recommended price positioning: **$50,000–$90,000** depending on scope.

### Managed Search Intelligence

An ongoing program that monitors demand, context, competitors, sources, selection conditions, interventions, and business impact while continuously executing priority improvements.

Recommended price positioning: **$15,000–$35,000 per month**.

### Agency Intelligence Partnership

A white-label, embedded, co-branded, or system-building relationship that gives an agency specialized Search Intelligence capability while protecting the agency-client relationship.

## 11. Buyer objections the site must answer

### “What do you actually do?”

Answer through the four-solution system and tangible outputs.

### “Is this just SEO?”

Explain that SEO is foundational but Hendricks connects demand, AI-mediated search, evidence, paid and organic acquisition, implementation, analytics, and business impact.

### “Is this GEO/AEO?”

Explain that AI-search visibility is part of the work, but mentions and citations alone do not answer commercial selection or business impact.

### “Can you guarantee ChatGPT recommends us?”

No. Explain controlled observations, the conditions Hendricks can improve, and evidence-based testing.

### “AI results are personalized. How can this be measured?”

Explain neutral, cohort, journey, platform, location, and time panels. Report distributions and stability rather than one universal ranking.

### “How do you know why a competitor won?”

State that Hendricks does not inspect hidden model reasoning. It studies output, citations, sources, brand associations, evidence, interventions, and changes.

### “Does this make money?”

Explain exposure, behavior, commercial, and causal measurement layers with evidence grades.

### “Will you replace our agency or team?”

Not by default. Hendricks can augment, coordinate, implement, or build systems others operate.
